# /brand-brief — Brand Brief

Captures the core brand context for any brand you work on. Works for every service — paid media, email marketing, SEO, content, creative production, strategy, or anything else.

**This is the minimum context needed before any work starts.**

Run it once per brand. Save the output. Everything downstream reads it.

---

## How to Run

Ask the questions below. You can collect answers in a conversation, from a client intake doc, or from a kickoff call transcript. Fill in what you have — mark anything unknown as `TBD` and flag it at the end.

---

## Questions to Ask

### 1. The Business
- What is the company name?
- What do they sell? (product, service, app — be specific)
- What is the price point? (or price range / packages)
- Where are they based? What markets do they operate in?
- How long have they been around? Are they a startup, scaling, or established?

### 2. The Customer
- Who is their primary customer? (be specific — not "everyone")
- What is that customer's biggest frustration or problem?
- What does that customer really want — the dream outcome?
- Any secondary customer segments worth knowing about?

### 3. The Brand
- How would you describe the brand voice in 3–5 words?
- What is the brand NOT? (what feels wrong or off-brand?)
- What is the one thing they want every piece of content or ad to communicate?
- Are there words, phrases, or topics that must be avoided?

### 4. What Makes Them Different
- What is genuinely different about this brand vs competitors?
- What do happy customers say about them? (pull from reviews if possible)
- Is there a guarantee, unique process, or proprietary method?

### 5. Current Marketing
- Which channels are they currently active on? (Meta, Google, TikTok, email, SEO, etc.)
- What is working well right now?
- What has been tried and failed?
- Are there any compliance rules or restrictions? (health claims, financial services, etc.)

### 6. Goals
- What is the primary goal for working with this system? (leads, sales, subscribers, installs, etc.)
- What does success look like in 90 days?
- Is there a specific KPI or target number?

## Output

Save the filled brief as `brand-brief.md` in the current project or client folder.

Use this format:

```markdown
# Brand Brief — [Client Name]

**Last updated:** [Date]
**Filled by:** [Your name]
**Services in scope:** [List]

---

## The Business
- **Name:** [X]
- **What they sell:** [X]
- **Price point:** [X]
- **Market:** [X]
- **Stage:** [Startup / Scaling / Established]

## The Customer
- **Primary customer:** [X]
- **Biggest frustration:** [X]
- **Dream outcome:** [X]
- **Other segments:** [X or none]

## The Brand
- **Voice:** [3–5 words]
- **Not:** [What feels wrong]
- **Core message:** [The one thing]
- **Avoid:** [Words / topics / angles]

## Differentiation
- **What's genuinely different:** [X]
- **What customers say:** [Key quote or theme from reviews]
- **Proof:** [Guarantee / method / credentials]

## Current Marketing
- **Active channels:** [List]
- **What's working:** [X]
- **What's failed:** [X]
- **Restrictions:** [X or none]

## Goals
- **Primary goal:** [X]
- **90-day success:** [X]
- **KPI / target:** [X]

---

## TBD / Missing Info
[List anything that couldn't be answered — flag for follow-up]
```
