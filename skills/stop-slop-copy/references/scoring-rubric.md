# Scoring Rubric — Overall Verdict for This Check

This is separate from individual findings. Individual findings point at specific lines. This score gives one overall verdict for the "does it sound human" half of this skill.

## Score the whole piece, 1-10 on each dimension

| Dimension | Ask |
|---|---|
| **Directness** | Does it state things, or announce them first? |
| **Rhythm** | Varied sentence length, or metronomic? Period-density under 1 per 10 words? |
| **Trust** | Does it respect the reader's intelligence, or over-explain/hand-hold? |
| **Authenticity** | Full sentences with real connectors, or stacked fragments? |
| **Density** | Is there anything left that could be cut without losing meaning? |

Add the five scores together, out of 50.

## Turning the score into a verdict

- **35/50 or above:** clean, or needs light polish only
- **Below 35/50:** needs another pass — treat as a blocker-level verdict for this check, even if no single finding on its own was severe. A pile of should-fix-level issues adds up to a piece that isn't ready.

## Automatic override, regardless of score

If period-density is above 1 per 8 words anywhere in the piece (excluding intentional ad-copy fragments, see the channel exception in `anti-slop-checklist.md`), that's an automatic "needs another pass" — no exceptions, no averaging it out with a good score elsewhere. That's not rhythm, that's staccato.

## How this combines with individual findings

The score is the headline for this check. The individual findings (quote/rule/why/fix) are what tells the requester exactly what to change. Report both — the score alone isn't actionable, and the findings alone don't tell the requester how close the whole piece is to done.
