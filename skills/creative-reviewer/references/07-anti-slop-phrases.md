# Word-Level Slop

Words and phrases that mark copy as machine-written. Bilingual: Dutch and English.

Scan for these before scoring anything else. They are the cheapest findings to make and the
cheapest to fix — every entry below can be deleted outright, and the sentence survives.

Companion file: `08-anti-slop-structures.md` covers sentence and paragraph patterns.

---

## 1. Throat-clearing

Copy that announces it is about to speak. Delete the whole phrase; the next sentence becomes the
opening line.

**Warm-up openers**

| EN | NL |
|---|---|
| "Here's the thing" | "Het punt is dit:" |
| "Here's what / this / that" | "Waar het om gaat is" |
| "The uncomfortable truth is" | "De ongemakkelijke waarheid is" |
| "It turns out" | "Het blijkt dat" |
| "The real [X] is" | "De echte [X] is" |
| "Let me be clear" | "Laat me duidelijk zijn" |
| "The truth is," | "De waarheid is," |
| "I'll say it again:" | "Ik zeg het nog een keer:" |
| "I'm going to be honest" | "Eerlijk gezegd" |
| "Can we talk about" | "Kunnen we het even hebben over" |

**Stage directions.** Copy narrating its own shape: "Hint:", "Plot twist:", "You already know this,
but", "Let me walk you through", "In this section we'll", "Let's dive in", "Here's what you need to
know". Identical pattern in Dutch. Start instead.

**Scene-setters.** "In today's [X]", "In a world where", "At its core", "At the end of the day",
"The reality is", "It's worth noting" / NL: "In de kern", "In een wereld waarin", "Het is het
vermelden waard", "Uiteindelijk".

## 2. Padding

Delete the word and reread. If nothing changed, it was padding.

**Adverbs.** Cut nearly all.

- EN: really, just, literally, genuinely, honestly, simply, actually, deeply, truly, fundamentally, interestingly, importantly, crucially
- NL: echt, gewoon, letterlijk, oprecht, eerlijk gezegd, simpelweg, eigenlijk, nogmaals, absoluut

**Emphasis crutches.** Instructions telling the reader how hard to feel something. Copy earns
emphasis; it does not request it.

- EN: "Full stop.", "Let that sink in.", "Make no mistake", "This matters because"
- NL: "Punt. Uit.", "Laat dat even bezinken.", "Vergis je niet", "En dat is precies waarom"

**Permission-granting.** "And that's okay", "And that's fine" / NL: "En dat is oké". Reassurance
nobody asked for.

**Lazy extremes.** every, always, never, everyone, no one / NL: iedereen, niemand, altijd, nooit.
An unearned absolute standing where a number or example belongs.

**Hedges.** most, many, some, often, tend to / NL: de meeste, veel, vaak, neigen naar. Either you
know the figure or you do not. Say which.

## 3. Borrowed vocabulary

Words chosen because they are everywhere in training data, not because they fit this sentence.

**Model favourites:** delve, crucial, intricate, pivotal, tapestry, testament, underscore, garner,
foster, enhance, align with, landscape (as abstract noun), robust, seamless, leverage, embrace,
unlock, empower, elevate.

**Business jargon**

| Avoid | Use instead | NL |
|---|---|---|
| Navigate (challenges) | Handle, solve | omgaan met |
| Unpack | Explain, examine | uitleggen |
| Landscape (context) | Situation, field | situatie |
| Gamechanger | Important | belangrijk |
| Double down on | Invest more in | meer inzetten op |
| Deep dive | Analysis, look | analyse |
| Stakeholders | The people involved | de betrokkenen |
| Impactful | Effective | effectief |

**Aphorism formulas.** "X is the Y of Z", "X is not a tool but a mirror" / NL: "X is niet een
[ding], maar een [ding]". The shape of insight with no claim inside. Write the claim.

---

## 4. Evasion

Language that looks like a claim but cannot be held to one.

**Unnamed specifics.** "The reasons are structural", "The implications are significant" / NL: "De
redenen zijn structureel", "De implicaties zijn aanzienlijk". Name it or cut the sentence.

**Dressed-up guesses.** "not extensively documented", "it is believed that", "likely" / NL: "men
gelooft dat", "waarschijnlijk". State what is unknown. Never style a guess as a finding.

**Telling instead of showing.** "It was difficult", "She felt insecure" / NL: "Het was moeilijk",
"Ze voelde zich onzeker". Write the scene that produces the feeling. In ad copy this is the single
most expensive miss — a named emotion is inert, an image is not.

**Synonym cycling.** The same thing renamed repeatedly to dodge repetition. Repetition is fine.
Reuse the word.

**Flattery.** "Great question!", "You're absolutely right". Never in marketing copy.

---

## Scanning order

1. Openers and stage directions — highest hit rate, zero judgement required
2. Adverbs and emphasis crutches
3. Borrowed vocabulary
4. Evasion — slowest to spot, most damaging to leave in
